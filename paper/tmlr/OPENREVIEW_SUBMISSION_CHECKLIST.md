# OpenReview Submission Checklist

## Author profiles

- [ ] Every author has one complete, active OpenReview profile.
- [ ] Current affiliation and institutional email are verified.
- [ ] Education and employment history are complete.
- [ ] Publication records are imported and deduplicated.
- [ ] Advisors, advisees, collaborators, and other conflicts are current.
- [ ] Every author has sufficient TMLR submission quota.
- [ ] Every author has approved the submitted manuscript and author order.

## Submission declarations

- [ ] Funding is reported accurately.
- [ ] Competing interests are reported accurately.
- [ ] Conflicts not captured by profile history are declared.
- [ ] Human-subjects/IRB response is marked not applicable if appropriate.
- [ ] Candidate Action Editors have been reviewed for expertise and conflicts.
- [ ] No overlapping archival or peer-reviewed submission is active.
- [ ] The authors accept that a non-desk-rejected submission and its review
      history may remain publicly visible on OpenReview.

## Anonymous upload

- [ ] Upload `kepler_paper_tmlr_v1_submission.pdf`.
- [ ] Upload `kepler_tmlr_supplement_v1.zip`.
- [ ] Do not upload the private repository URL.
- [ ] Do not upload Git metadata, checkpoints, local logs, or unsanitized
      result JSON.
- [ ] Confirm the PDF shows “Anonymous authors” and the double-blind header.
- [ ] Confirm the first-page LLM assistance disclosure is visible.
- [ ] Confirm the supplemental ZIP is below 100 MB.
- [ ] Download the uploaded files from OpenReview and compare their SHA-256
      values with the preparation report before clicking final submit.

## Final human review

- [ ] A general ML reader can explain the contribution after pages 1–2.
- [ ] A methods reader accepts the inference unit, exact test, and causal
      wording.
- [ ] A learning-dynamics reader finds the grokking, curriculum, and
      mechanistic-interpretability positioning fair.
- [ ] Authors have checked every OpenReview metadata field and the final
      author list.
